Why the last one did not stick
You have tried a budgeting app before.
They budget by calendar month.
Your money works in salary cycles.
A month that starts on the 1st is meaningless when you are paid on the 25th. Mizaniya's cycle runs the 25th to the 24th, because that is when money arrives.
They cannot hold a rent lump sum.
₦900,000 due once a year is not a monthly bill, and treating it as one makes every other figure on the screen wrong.
They only track what you owe,
not what you are owed.
You lent a colleague ₦40,000. That is real money, and it is missing from every app you have tried.

What we can and cannot see
We never store your bank data in readable form.
If you connect a bank, the movement reaches our server before it is encrypted — that is how it gets to you. We will not tell you otherwise, because you are deciding whether to trust us with a salary and a sentence that sounds better than the truth is not worth what it costs.
Encrypted, with the keys held outside the database.
Every access to production data is logged, and you can ask for that record.
Bank access is read-only — it can see money move, it cannot move money.
None of this applies unless you connect a bank, and nothing connects one but you. Mizaniya never holds or moves money — it can see that ₦12,000.00 left your account and ask which envelope it belongs to. That is the whole of it.

The page is still built from real components at their real size — but from fragments, never from whole screens. A 1,400px phone screen in a column beside 300px of copy is a screenshot dump: the reader gets an app they will not read against prose they will, and the two do not relate. So §2.3 is a composition — the gauge and its figure, with Needs attention overlapping its corner, because the second card is the reason for the first and on a phone you have to scroll to find it. §2.8 is five components in a quincunx around the one that explains them. Fragments overlap; none is tilted, faded, or cut through its own content — that is what keeps this from being the device-frames-drifting-in-space the brief rules out.
