The rules that never bend
·Every amount carries its kobo; the naira sign is part of the figure.
·Money is tabular and right-aligned. No bare minus sign, ever.
·Spendable figures round down; obligations round up.
·Red means money going wrong, and nothing else.
·One colour for the mark. The Arabic never carries a figure.
