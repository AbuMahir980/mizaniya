§08, and the flow starts here. The password rule is stated before the field, not after rejection, and it is one rule: twelve characters. Consent is a tick now. It read creating an account means you have read what we hold and why — a claim about the reader, made on their behalf, in grey text above a button. Consent you have to reach for is consent; a sentence asserting you already gave it is not. It sits above the button, because this is the moment a person hands something over and a line below the action is a line nobody reads.

The trust panel is gone. It carried four claim blocks on every auth screen, and §10.1 fixes that wording — a fixed wording repeated in five places is a wording that will differ in one of them, and it did: the forbidden sentence reached that panel twice. What stays is the one §10.1 claim, required here and character-identical to the landing page, and a link. The three supporting claims live in one modal (board 21).
