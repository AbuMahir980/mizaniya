The number steps, the cycle sweeps. A figure counting down on scroll is unreadable at every position but the ends, which motion.md §0.2 forbids and §9 extends to this page. Instead every anchor is a real value from a real payday, fully rendered; the figure snaps between them and the rail sweeps. The first three panels are one section at three scroll positions, the third with motion off — there is no fallback layout, only emphasis removed, so nothing is gated behind the animation. Treatment 3 is honest about a gap: safe-to-spend has two anchors in the seed, not four, and the two it lacks are drawn as gaps rather than closed up.
