The state most people are in, and it was drawn as a failure. The board read “we can’t work out an estimate yet” with Nisab — not entered, while seed-data.md has carried a nisab of ₦2,450,000.00 and a result — below the nisab, no zakat estimated — since 10 September. It also printed Hawl start — 25 Sep 2026, which is the cycle start; the seed says 16 June 2026, 1 Muharram 1448. Two wrong figures on a four-row table. Repo rule 2 is usually about inventing a number; this is its other half — a number that exists and was not read.

The nisab is looked up now, not asked for. The owner: “if you are telling an individual to look it up and enter it today — we should be the one searching that up.” He is right, and it is also why the screen was thin: with no threshold there is no answer, so it could only ever be a form with a caveat attached.

Where you stand is a threshold, not a progress bar. The fill is neutral and the copy says so — nobody is working towards owing zakat, and an emerald rail filling towards the nisab would say they were. It is there because the nisab moves with the price of silver: below is a distance rather than a verdict, and someone under the line this month can be over it next month without earning a naira more.

What was left out, and why, is what makes the rest believable. Three of those four lines are positions the app took on the owner’s behalf, and a person cannot check a decision they cannot see. The caveat then names those same three, so check with someone qualified has something to be about.
