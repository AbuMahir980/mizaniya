Security and privacy
We never store your bank data in readable form.
Everything below is what actually happens, including the parts that are not flattering. Each answer carries the caveat that goes with it, in the same size as the answer.
What we hold, and in what form
Your budget — categories, amounts, movements, debts — is encrypted at rest, and the keys are held outside the database. A copy of the database on its own is not a copy of your money.
Without an account, none of this applies: your budget is in this browser, it is not encrypted, and anyone who can sign in to the device can open it. That is the trade the free tier makes, and it is why an account exists.
What a bank connection can and cannot do
Read-only. It can see that ₦12,000.00 left your account and ask which envelope it belongs to. It cannot move money, and it is off until you turn it on.
The movement reaches our server before it is encrypted — that is how it gets to you. We will not tell you otherwise, because you are deciding whether to trust us with a salary and a sentence that sounds better than the truth is not worth what it costs.
Who can look at production data
Every access is logged, and you can ask us for that record at any time. The log names the person, the time and what they opened.
People can read production data. Anyone who tells you otherwise is describing a company that cannot fix a bug.
Leaving, and taking everything with you
Export whenever you like, in a file you can read without us — not a backup format, a file you can open in a spreadsheet. Deleting your account deletes the data, not just the login.
A copy may persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before those expire. We cannot read it and we cannot restore it selectively.
Passwords
Twelve characters or more, and that is the whole rule. No symbol classes, no expiry, no reuse check — every one of those makes people write passwords down. Setting a new one signs every other device out.
We never see it. If you lose it we cannot recover your budget for you; we can only let you set a new password for the account it belongs to.
None of this applies unless you connect a bank, and nothing connects one but you.
