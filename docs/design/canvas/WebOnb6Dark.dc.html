Step 6 of 6. The owner asked for the deduction to be shown, and he was right twice: it was not shown, and it was wrong. The card said ₦85,000.00 a payday from ₦305,000.00 already saved and five paydays — three figures, none of which belongs to onboarding. seed-data solves this sum explicitly for step 6, and the 360 screen has quoted it correctly all along: 900,000 − 400,000 = 500,000 still to find, ÷ 6 paydays, rounded up, = ₦83,333.34. Rounded up because a rate rounded down reaches ₦499,999.98 and misses, and falling short is not something a figure may do quietly.
