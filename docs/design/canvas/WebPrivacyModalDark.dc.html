What we hold, and what we never ask for.
✕
Four things, each with a reason, and the list of what we never ask you for.
We hold four things, and each one has a reason
Your email, to sign you in. Your budget, because it is the product. Your devices, so you can sign a lost phone out. A bank connection, only if you turn one on.
We never ask for a card number, a BVN or a phone number
Payment goes straight to the processor — we do not see the number and do not store it, not even the last four digits.
Bank access is read-only
It can see money move. It cannot move money. Connecting a bank is optional and off until you turn it on.
