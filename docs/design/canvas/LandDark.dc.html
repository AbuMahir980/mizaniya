The brief's nine sections in order, and the order is the argument. Revised 25 September after review. The page is still built from real components at their real size — but from fragments, never from whole screens. A 1,400px phone screen in a column beside 300px of copy is a screenshot dump: the reader gets an app they will not read against prose they will, and the two do not relate. So §2.3 is a composition — the gauge and its figure, with Needs attention overlapping its corner, because the second card is the reason for the first and on a phone you have to scroll to find it. §2.8 is five components in a quincunx around the one that explains them. Fragments overlap; none is tilted, faded, or cut through its own content — that is what keeps this from being the device-frames-drifting-in-space the brief rules out. Two image slots are specified, not filled: photography belongs in the problem, never beside a figure.
