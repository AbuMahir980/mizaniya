Mizaniya (ميزانية) comes from mizan (ميزان) — a set of scales. The beam is drawn one notch off level, because the app exists to tell you whether today is in balance, and most days it is not quite.
Drawn on a 48-unit grid. Keep clear space of one pan-height all round.
Beam tips 9° — enough to read as motion, not as an error.
Stroke 3.8 at 48, scaled with the mark.
One colour. Never two, never filled.

Mizaniya (ميزانية, “budget”) comes from mizan (ميزان) — a set of scales, and the mark is one, tipped. Locked at the owner’s pick from three candidates; the meem monogram and the balance bar are retired. This mark is the app icon, the favicon, the sidebar lockup and the letterhead on the printed debt record.
