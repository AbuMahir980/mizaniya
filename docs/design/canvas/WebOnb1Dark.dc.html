Step 1 of 6, and skippable. The owner asked whether this step is needed at all now that people can sign up — and the sign-up form answers it: it takes an email address and a password, nothing else. There is no name to inherit, and the alternative is heading a paper record with an email address. But the old question, what should we call you?, promised a greeting Mizaniya never gives — no name on Home, none in a report. So it asks what the other party should see, and says that a kunya or a household name does the job. The document is the 1440 treatment: at 360 a preview would push the field off the screen, so the phone gets the sentence and this board shows where the answer lands.
