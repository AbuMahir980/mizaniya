Focus ring
Focused
3px ring, rgba(78,203,139,.34), outside the border and never clipped. Identical on every focusable component, keyboard and pointer alike. It is the one place the accent appears on a non-primary control.
Touch target
Every tappable thing clears 44 × 44 at 360px, including table-row actions, the switch, and the close ✕ on a sheet. The icon inside may be 20px; the target is not.
Four hues carry meaning and nothing else carries any: emerald for the primary action and money going right, ochre for near-a-limit, slate for people and neutral data, rose for money going wrong. Everything else is the neutral ramp.
